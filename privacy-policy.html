Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. At Al-Arabiyah Learning Center, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Student information (name, age, course preferences)
  • Payment transaction details
  • Class attendance and progress data

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our classes
  • To communicate about schedules and updates
  • To process payments and issue certificates
  • To send notifications and support messages

3. Data Protection

We implement security measures to protect your personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform.

5. Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Opt out of communications
  • Contact us with concerns

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via WhatsApp at +8801737326555 or email at info@alarabiyahlearningcenter.com.
